#+TITLE: OBTF Journal Styles — Emacs Org

Use these as starting points, not rules. Pick one style and keep writing in chronological order.

* Minimal Daily

#+begin_example
* 2026-01-16
- Walked by the water.
- Paid electrician.
- Felt clear in the afternoon. #clarity
#+end_example

* Daily Check-In

#+begin_example
* 2026-01-16

Energy:
Body:
Mind:
Work:
Home:
One thing noticed:
#+end_example

* Longform Reflection

#+begin_example
* 2026-01-16

What happened:

What I noticed:

What may matter later:
#+end_example

* Weekly Signals

#+begin_example
* Week of 2026-01-12

Body:
Mind:
Work:
Relationships:

#+end_example

* Field Notes

#+begin_example
* 2026-01-16 — Place

Weather:

What changed:
What I want to remember:
#+end_example

* Creative Seeds

#+begin_example
* 2026-01-16

#essay-seed
#+end_example
