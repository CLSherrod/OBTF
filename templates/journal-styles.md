# OBTF Journal Styles — Markdown

Use these as starting points, not rules. Pick one style and keep writing in chronological order.

---

## Minimal Daily

```markdown
## 2026-01-16
- Walked by the water.
- Paid electrician.
- Felt clear in the afternoon. #clarity
```

---

## Daily Check-In

```markdown
## 2026-01-16

Energy:

Mind:
Work:

One thing noticed:
```

---

## Longform Reflection

